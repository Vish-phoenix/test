Delete your Hem Industries account

Use this page to request deletion of your account and associated personal data. We’ll verify your request and permanently remove or anonymize the data described below.

How to request deletion (3 quick steps)

  1. Sign in to the app with the account you want deleted (helps us verify ownership).
  2. Submit the form below — include the email/username associated with the account and a brief reason. Alternatively, click the email link if you prefer to request deletion by email.
  3. We’ll verify your identity and confirm the deletion timeline by email. Once verification completes we will start deletion within the timeframe below.

If you cannot sign in, you can still request deletion by emailing support@yourdomain.com. We’ll ask for a form of ID or additional verification only when necessary to prevent fraudulent deletion.

Account deletion request form

If the form does not work, email: support@yourdomain.com

What will be deleted — and what we may keep

Immediately deleted (user-accessible)
Account profile (name, email, username), profile photo, user preferences, direct messages and private chat content (unless legally required to keep), and personalized settings.
Deleted after verification / processing
Uploaded files and media (images, documents), saved payment instruments (note: payment processors may keep limited data for transactional recordkeeping), order history (may be anonymized), and user-generated content where requested.
Data kept / retained for legal or operational reasons
We may retain:
- Transaction records and billing logs for financial / legal compliance: retention: 6 years (or your local legal requirement).
- Server logs and backups for security and fraud detection: retention: up to 90 days (backups may persist longer in immutable archives but will be rendered inaccessible and masked).
- Aggregated/anonymized analytics data used for product improvements (non-identifying): indefinite.

If you need a tighter retention period for any retained data due to special circumstances, contact us at support@yourdomain.com.

Verification & deletion timeline

Verification
We will acknowledge receipt within 3 business days and may ask you to confirm ownership (via email link or a short verification step).
Deletion completion
After successful verification we will perform deletion within 30 days. Some data (backups, logs, legal records) may remain as described above.
Final confirmation
We will send a confirmation email when deletion is complete and include a summary of what was removed.
